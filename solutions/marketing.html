Frequently asked questions

Which attribution model does Funnelsight use?

U-shaped by default: 40% of the credit goes to the first touch, 40% to the touch that created the opportunity, and 20% is shared among the touches in between. An admin can switch to first touch, last touch or linear, compare two models side by side before switching, and every change is logged.

Can we add costs that aren't in ad platforms, like events or partners?

Yes. Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Meta Ads and Microsoft Ads connect directly. Event budgets, partner commissions or agency fees are imported from a spreadsheet or entered by hand, and each channel's return then includes them.

Do we need to change how campaigns are tagged?

Not to start. Funnelsight reads the UTM parameters and click IDs you already use. The data check lists the visits and leads that arrive without a source, so tagging can be fixed where it changes the numbers most.
