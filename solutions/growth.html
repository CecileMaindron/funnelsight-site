Frequently asked questions

Does Funnelsight replace our product analytics tool?

It works alongside it. Your product analytics tool keeps recording events, funnels inside the product and experiments. Funnelsight reads the events that matter for the funnel, such as sign-up, activation and upgrade, and connects them with the acquisition source, the CRM and billing.

Do we need a data team to set it up?

A growth marketer or a growth PM can do it. They connect the product analytics tool, the CRM and the billing tool, and agree on the activation event with the product team. Before the first chart, a data check shows what can't be linked yet, such as sign-ups without a source.

Can it read accounts, not just users?

Yes. Each user is linked to an account, by email domain and by the company in your CRM, so activation, conversion and revenue can be read per account as well as per user. Personal email addresses and subsidiaries show up in the data check, so they can be fixed.
