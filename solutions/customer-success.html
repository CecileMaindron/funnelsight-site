For customer success teams

Know your accounts before the renewal, or a ticket, tells you.

Most accounts will never have a dedicated CSM. Funnelsight brings product usage and CRM data together on every account, so the ones that need a person get one in time, and the rest can be managed on data.

Works with your CRM and your product analytics.

"Which accounts need me this week?"

One list for the week, including the accounts nobody owns.

Rules your team sets flag a drop in active users, a plan limit reached or a main contact who stopped logging in. Each flag goes to whoever owns the account in your setup, a CSM, a renewal manager or an account executive, and accounts without an owner are flagged the same way. A flag starts a conversation: an account with no usage may simply be waiting for its integrator.

"What changed since the last call?"

Walk into every call knowing what moved in the account.

Funnelsight lists what changed in the account: who joined, who stopped, which team started using the product, which onboarding emails they acted on and which meetings are booked. Dated notes keep the project and the pain points next to the data, so whoever picks up the account next starts from the same place.

"Where can this account grow?"

See the room to grow, team by team.

The renewal is only part of the job. Seats bought against seats used, and the teams already reaching for a higher plan, show where an expansion conversation can start, from what people already do in the product.
