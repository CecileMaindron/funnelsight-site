Only part of your customers will ever have a dedicated CSM. Funnelsight brings product usage and CRM data together on every account, so customer and lifecycle marketing can reach the rest with the right message, and pass the accounts that need a person to CS in time.

Works with your CRM, your product analytics and your email platform.

"Which customers should get which email?"

Send lifecycle emails on what each account does, not on its signing date.

A customer signed six weeks ago can be using the product every day or not at all. Funnelsight groups accounts by what they do in the product, from rules your team sets, so each group gets the message that fits it. Some situations call for a person rather than an email, such as a main contact who stopped logging in: those accounts go to their owner in CS.

"Did our emails change anything?"

See whether each email changes what it was meant to.

Each email has a behavior it should change: an upgrade after an email about the plan limit, a first report after an onboarding email. Funnelsight compares that behavior between the accounts that got the email and a small group held back from the same rule, so the gain isn't confused with what would have happened anyway.

"Where can this account grow?"

Spot the accounts about to outgrow their plan.

Plans are counted in seats, credits, records or projects. When usage gets close to what the plan includes, or a team asks for access it doesn't have, an expansion conversation can start from what people already do. Funnelsight shows it account by account, so an upgrade campaign reaches the right people, or the account goes to its owner when the deal is big enough for a conversation.

Frequently asked questions

Does Funnelsight send the emails?

Your email or marketing automation platform keeps sending them. Funnelsight shows which accounts match each rule, from product usage and CRM data, and measures what changed after each send.

Does it replace our customer success platform?

It works alongside it. Health scores, tasks and playbooks stay where your CS team runs them. Funnelsight adds where each account came from and what marketing sent it, next to what it does in the product.

How do you know an email made a difference?

Hold back a small share of the accounts that match the rule. Funnelsight compares what both groups do in the product over the following weeks, so the effect isn't confused with customers who would have acted anyway.
