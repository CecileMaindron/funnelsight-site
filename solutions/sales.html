"I've been asked to follow up this lead. What do I need to know?"

Every activity written so a rep understands it in five seconds.

A raw event name means nothing to someone who joined the team last month. Funnelsight writes each activity in plain words, with where it happened (in the trial, on a form, at an event), the product it relates to and a link to the content. The product interest can then point to the email sequence your marketing ops team prepared for it.

"Which accounts are moving that nobody has flagged?"

Find the accounts waking up below the thresholds.

Some accounts never reach the MQL or PQL bar, because each activity scores too low on its own. Others are parked in "not now" and can't change status for weeks. Funnelsight adds up the small signals at account level, such as several people from the same company on the site or more trial sign-ups than usual, and writes them on the account. A rep or BDR can reach out before the status catches up.

User-level vs account-level analytics

"Another alert nobody reads?"

Fewer signals, each one proven.

A signal reaches reps only once it has shown it changes something. Funnelsight first compares it with the average, before any rep sees it, then a few reps try it, then the whole team, and it stays monitored: a signal that stops making a difference is removed. That comparison is what sales leadership needs to see before asking every rep to act on it.
