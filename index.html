Questions worth asking before you start.

What does Funnelsight actually do?

Funnelsight is marketing reporting and attribution software for B2B SaaS teams. It follows each customer from the first anonymous visit to paying customer and renewal, shows your results against target, and where the funnel slows down when a target is missed. It connects to GA4, Google Ads and your CRM, without replacing them.

What data sources can I connect?

Your CRM, product analytics, Google Analytics (GA4), and the ad platforms most B2B teams use: Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Meta Ads and Microsoft Ads. Costs no connected tool holds, like partner commissions or events, can be imported or entered by hand. No analytics tool in place yet? Import a spreadsheet to try Funnelsight, then connect a live source later.

Do I need a data team to set it up?

No. A marketing or growth team can connect its sources and get a working dashboard the same day. If you have a data analyst, they stay in charge of architecture, pipelines and integrations Funnelsight doesn't support natively, and often help set the shared rules once. After that, marketing and sales stop waiting for a new report for every question.

How is this different from exporting to a spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet answers the question once and has to be rebuilt the following week. Once a live source is connected, Funnelsight keeps the same view up to date, with the same rules applied every time.

Is my data secure, and where is it stored?

Your data stays in your workspace and is hosted in the EU: Funnelsight is based in Nantes, France. Nothing is shared beyond your account, and you can disconnect a source at any time.

Do I need a credit card to start the trial?

No. The trial runs 14 days, ends automatically, and there's nothing to cancel.
