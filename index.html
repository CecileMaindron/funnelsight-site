Questions worth asking before you start.

What does Funnelsight actually do?

One dashboard for the full customer journey, from acquisition source to expansion, so marketing and revenue work from the same numbers instead of separate exports.

Do I need a data team to set it up?

Not to get started. Connect your product analytics tool and your CRM yourself, and get a working dashboard the same day. A data team still helps with complex architecture or native integrations. That role isn't replaced. You're just not blocked without one.

What data sources can I connect?

Product analytics, your CRM, and acquisition sources like Google Analytics (GA4) and Google Ads. No analytics tool in place yet? Import a spreadsheet to try Funnelsight, then connect a live source later.

How is this different from just exporting to a spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet you rebuild every week answers the question once and needs redoing next week. Once a live source is connected, Funnelsight keeps the same view current automatically.

Does Funnelsight score or predict which accounts will churn or convert?

It flags accounts using rules you set, like a drop in usage or an activation threshold being crossed, and shows the resulting PQL or SQL score next to your CRM data. The rules stay visible and editable. It's not a black-box prediction.

Can other people on my team use it too?

Yes. Invite teammates from marketing and revenue, share a view for review, or export it for your quarterly business review, no rebuilding required.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Your data stays in your workspace, nothing is shared beyond your account, and you can disconnect a source anytime.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU. Funnelsight is based in Nantes, France, and data is hosted accordingly.

Do I need a credit card to start the trial?

No. The trial runs 14 days, ends automatically, and there's nothing to cancel.
