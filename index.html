Questions worth asking before you start.

What does Funnelsight actually do?

Funnelsight is marketing attribution software for B2B SaaS teams. It follows each customer from the first anonymous visit to paying customer and renewal, and compares sources on the revenue they bring and how fast they pay back. It works on top of the tools you already use, like GA4, Google Ads and your CRM.

Which channel gets the credit for a sale?

Usually more than one. A customer might read an article, click an ad and talk to sales before buying, and no tool can say for certain which touch made the difference. That's why Funnelsight talks about influence rather than cause. Your team picks the attribution template that fits how you sell, and Funnelsight shares the credit for each sale across the touches on its path. And a source isn't judged on a single measure, such as the number of net new leads, the total volume of contacts or the last touch before the opportunity is created, but on how its contacts convert at each step: what share of them activate, then buy, then renew.

Why don't our attribution numbers match what sales reports?

Because they answer different questions. Sales reads the pipeline deal by deal, each deal counted whole. An attribution model splits each deal across the touches that came before it, so a channel's figure adds up shares of many deals and matches none of them. Attribution is built to compare channels, not to rebuild totals. Funnelsight shows both readings side by side, with your CRM as the reference for pipeline and revenue.

What data sources can I connect?

Product analytics, your CRM, and acquisition sources like Google Analytics (GA4) and Google Ads. No analytics tool in place yet? Import a spreadsheet to try Funnelsight, then connect a live source later.

Do I need a data team to set it up?

No. A marketing or growth team can connect its sources and get a working dashboard the same day. A data team only becomes useful for complex architecture or integrations Funnelsight doesn't support natively.

We already have a data analyst. Does Funnelsight replace them?

No. Funnelsight doesn't take over data architecture, pipelines or integrations beyond what it supports natively, and your analyst stays in charge of those. What changes is that marketing and sales no longer wait for a new report for every question. Framework rules are set once, often with your analyst, and each team builds its own views on the same numbers.

How is this different from exporting to a spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet answers the question once and has to be rebuilt the following week. Once a live source is connected, Funnelsight keeps the same view up to date, with the same rules applied every time.

Does Funnelsight score or predict which accounts will churn or convert?

It flags accounts using rules you set, like a drop in usage or an activation threshold being crossed, and shows the resulting PQL or SQL score next to your CRM data. The rules stay visible and editable. It's not a black-box prediction.

Can other people on my team use it too?

Yes. Invite teammates from marketing, sales and customer success. Admins set up sources and shared rules, editors build and share dashboards, and viewers follow them and add notes.

Is my data secure, and where is it stored?

Your data stays in your workspace and is hosted in the EU: Funnelsight is based in Nantes, France. Nothing is shared beyond your account, and you can disconnect a source at any time.
