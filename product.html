"Will next quarter's pipeline be enough?"

See the pipeline gap while there's still time to close it.

Funnelsight compares the pipeline created this quarter with its target, and forecasts where the current pace leads. You see a gap with weeks left to act, not at the review.

Each source is compared with its own target to date, so you know where the gap comes from.

"Where do our leads stall?"

See where each source's leads drop off.

Funnelsight follows each lead from its first visit to the deal signed in your CRM. For every source, you see how many leads became opportunities, how many became customers, and the step where most of them stop.

Paid search brings plenty of leads that rarely turn into opportunities. Events bring opportunities that rarely close. The two problems call for different fixes, and a lead count alone shows neither.

"Can we trust this number?"

A data check before the first chart.

Like a site health report, the data check lists each blind spot with its weight, so you know what to fix first and what to keep in mind while you read. It never blocks anything.

"Why don't our two reports match?"

Shared rules, and every change logged.

The attribution model, the window and the definitions are set once and apply to every campaign. When a rule changes, the change is logged with its date and author, and past periods are recalculated or the break is clearly marked. Funnelsight also shows how credit moves between sources after a change, so every team can see what it gained or lost.

"Why did sign-ups drop that week?"

Notes that keep the context.

Anyone can add a dated note to a chart, such as a campaign launch, a site migration or a tracking bug and its fix. The note stays with that week, so the explanation is still there months later.

When a rule fixes an error after the fact, such as bot sign-ups or a mistagged campaign, past weeks are corrected, the bar is hatched and the change is logged. The daily, planning and leadership views all read the same numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to get started?

Your CRM, GA4, your product analytics tool and your ad platforms, or even a spreadsheet to start. The first views and the data check are ready on the first day. Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Meta Ads and Microsoft Ads connect natively, and costs such as partner commissions or events are imported from a spreadsheet or entered by hand. Revenue and payback by source follow once your costs are in.

Can we change the attribution model later?

Yes. An admin changes it, the change is logged, and past periods are recalculated or the break is clearly marked. You can also compare two models side by side before you switch.

Does Funnelsight replace our analytics tools or our CRM?

No. It works on top of them. Your analytics tools keep collecting, the CRM stays the reference for pipeline and revenue, and Funnelsight connects what they record into one view of the funnel.
