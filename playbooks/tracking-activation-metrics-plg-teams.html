Tracking activation metrics

PLG teams collect plenty of signup data, but activation, the moment a user actually gets value, is harder to pin down. It gets defined one way in the product team's dashboard and another way in growth's spreadsheet, and by the time everyone agrees on what counts, the metric is already out of date.

When activation lives in three different tools

A product manager tracks feature adoption in the analytics tool. A growth marketer builds cohort views in a spreadsheet pulled from raw exports. Sales looks at login frequency as a rough proxy for interest. None of these numbers agree with each other, and nobody wants to be the one to point that out in the weekly meeting. So activation gets defined once, in a slide, and then never revisited, even as the product keeps changing underneath it.

Common mistake: reacting to three disagreeing numbers by adding a fourth dashboard to reconcile them, instead of assigning one owned definition that every team actually uses. It's one of the easiest traps to fall into in PLG teams: tracking more instead of aligning on what's already there.

The math behind activation rate

Once a team agrees on a single activation event, the calculation is straightforward:

Formula

Activation rate (%) = (number of users who completed the activation event ÷ total new signups) × 100

The formula isn't the hard part. Getting three tools to agree on the numerator is. That's the actual problem Funnelsight solves, not the arithmetic.

How Funnelsight connects activation to the rest of the funnel

Funnelsight lets you define an activation event directly from product usage data, the same event tracking described on the product overview, and follow it through to trial conversion and revenue without exporting anything to a spreadsheet. You set the event once, and it's the same definition every dashboard downstream uses, including the activation and retention dashboard your team already looks at. No data team required to wire it up.

A concrete example

Say a team decides activation means a new account has connected at least one data source and invited a second teammate within the first five days. In Funnelsight, that's built as a single funnel step, not a formula split across two tools and reconciled by hand. From there, the team can see, without asking anyone to pull a report, what share of trials hit that step each week and whether that share is moving in the right direction. What counts as an activation event?

Whatever signals real use of your product, not just a signup. Most PLG teams pick something like a first key action, an invited teammate, or a connected integration.

Do I need engineering help to set this up?

No. Funnelsight is built for teams without a dedicated data team, and activation events are configured from existing product usage data, not custom tracking code.
