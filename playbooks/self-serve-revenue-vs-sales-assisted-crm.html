The self-serve customers your sales team can't see

A rep opens a target account and finds a prospect with no open opportunity. Five people from that company joined a trial at different times, in three separate trials, and the CRM can't say what happened next.

One account, two views

The five people did reach the CRM, each with a status, but none of those statuses says whether the person used the product, is still testing it or already pays. Funnelsight groups product usage and billing by account and puts each trial's status next to what the CRM holds.

What the rep can do with it

Taken one at a time, none of these prospects justified a sales follow-up. Grouped by account and read with product usage, they show a company where one team has paid for months and is outgrowing its plan, while another is testing right now. Some teams call this a product qualified account: several active users in one company, rather than one promising user.

That's worth a conversation. If finance bought for its own division, the rep can bring the product to the others, starting with marketing while its trial is running. If the purchase covers the whole company, the conversation is an upgrade, prompted by the higher-plan feature finance used every day of its preview. Either way, the rep opens with what these teams already do in the product, rather than a generic introduction.

The account can also fall between two segmentations. Sales sees a group with 2,000 employees, while customer success sees a few thousand dollars a year and no reason to assign anyone, so nobody follows it until both read the same view.

The signals worth sending to the CRM

Syncing every product event into the CRM would bury reps in data nobody keeps up to date. The signals worth sending are the few tied to decisions sales and customer success already make: a trial worth helping, a team ready to pay or upgrade, another division to approach, an account whose usage is falling. Funnelsight reads product, billing and CRM data together and sends those signals to the account in the CRM, where reps already work.
